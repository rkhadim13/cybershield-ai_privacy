Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 16, 2026

If you wish to request the deletion of your data from the CyberShield AI project, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Data Deletion

To initiate the deletion process, please send an email to rkhadim13@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Information to Include

Please include the following information in your email:

  • The platform (Instagram or Facebook Messenger) you used.
  • Your username or user ID on that platform.
  • Approximate date of last interaction.

Processing Time

We will process your request within 30 days of receiving your email. Once processed, all your data will be permanently deleted from our systems.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at rkhadim13@gmail.com.
